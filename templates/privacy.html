Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

Your privacy is a core principle of GrahaLogic. This policy explains exactly what data we collect, what we store, and — equally important — what we deliberately do not retain.

Our core commitment: Your individual questions to the AI and the AI's responses to those questions are never stored in our database. Only anonymized telemetry counts are retained for usage tracking.

1. What We Collect via Google Sign-In

When you sign in with Google, we receive:

  • Your Google account ID (internal identifier only)
  • Your name and email address
  • Your profile picture URL

We do not request access to your Google Drive, Gmail, Contacts, or any other Google service.

3. What We Explicitly Do Not Store

We have deliberately built GrahaLogic to not retain the following:

  • The text of any question you ask the AI (career questions, health questions, relationship questions, etc.)
  • The AI's individual answers to your follow-up questions
  • Any chat conversation history beyond the current browser session
  • Your IP address beyond standard server request logs (which rotate and are not linked to your profile)
  • Device fingerprinting or behavioral tracking data

4. How Your Data Is Used

We use your data solely to operate GrahaLogic:

  • Generating birth charts and AI readings
  • Sending transactional emails (reading ready notifications, weekly forecasts) if you have a saved chart
  • Tracking aggregate usage counts for product improvement (never individual content)
  • Enforcing fair-use limits (GrahaGems)

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI models.

5. Third-Party Services

GrahaLogic uses the following third-party services, each bound by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In — authentication only
  • Google Gemini / Vertex AI — processes your chart data to generate readings; Google's data processing terms apply
  • SendGrid — transactional email delivery only; your email address is transmitted to send you emails
  • Supabase (PostgreSQL) — database hosting; data is stored in US-based servers
  • Google Cloud Run — application hosting

6. Data Security

Your data is stored in a private PostgreSQL database with access restricted to the application. We use HTTPS for all communication. Birth data is stored as structured JSON and is only accessible to your own account — not to other users.

The admin (GrahaLogic operator) can see: total user counts, chart counts, and usage telemetry. The admin cannot see your individual AI questions or conversation content.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing support@grahalogic.ai. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

Anonymized telemetry counts (question counts) may be retained in aggregate form after account deletion.

8. Children's Privacy

GrahaLogic is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. We will always update the "Last updated" date and, for material changes, notify users via email. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests: support@grahalogic.ai
